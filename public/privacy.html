Privacy Policy

Sunny Star Adventure · last updated 14 November 2026

Sunny Star Adventure is a game for children. We built it to collect as little information as possible. This page explains what the game stores, what leaves your device, and why.

What we do not collect

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, voice or location.
  • No accounts or logins.
  • No free-text chat, and nothing typed in the game is ever sent to other people. Children type Bible memory verses, which are checked on the device. A teacher or parent can type in a verse of their own. It stays on that device, unless the grown-up chooses to share a class link (see "Verse classes").
  • No analytics or tracking scripts of our own.

What is saved on your device

The game saves progress in your browser's local storage, which stays on your device: unlocked levels, high score, coins, items bought with coins, jobs completed, the character's look and made-up name, the chosen pet, the sound and easy-mode settings, any verses a grown-up picked or typed in, and which verses have been memorized. Clear your browser's site data to erase all of it.

Online play with friends

Online play is optional. When a child makes or joins a room, the game connects directly to the other players' browsers using WebRTC. A free public connection service (PeerJS) helps the browsers find each other.

To connect, each browser and the connection service can see the other side's IP address, as with any internet connection. The game only sends gameplay data: positions, the made-up name, the outfit, stickers and which stars were collected. Nothing is stored on a server.

Verse classes

A teacher or parent can pick memory verses and start a class room. Children who join with the room code receive the list of chosen verses, sent as numbers that point into the game's built-in verse library, never as typed text. When a child types a verse from memory, the teacher's screen shows that child's made-up name with a tick beside the verse. This goes directly between the browsers in the room, is not stored on any server, and disappears when the class ends.

A teacher can also copy a class link and send it to families themselves, for example by email. The chosen verses, including any the teacher typed in, are written into the link's address. A device that opens the link reads the verses from the address; they are not sent through the game.

Hosting

The game's files are served by a web host. Like any website, the host may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security and reliability.

Advertising

The game may show ads at natural breaks between levels, through an ad provider: Google AdSense (H5 Games Ads), or the ad service of the game portal you are playing on. Every ad request is tagged as directed to children and non-personalized, so the provider is told not to use interest-based advertising or remarketing.

Ad providers may still use limited technical information, such as the IP address, browser type and a frequency-cap counter, to deliver ads, limit how often they appear, and prevent fraud. See the provider's own privacy policy, for example how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Children's privacy (COPPA and GDPR)

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents can delete everything saved on the device at any time by clearing the browser's site data for this game. Because we have no accounts and keep no player records, there is nothing stored on our side to review or delete.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. For questions, please contact us through the game's support page.
