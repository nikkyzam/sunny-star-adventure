For parents and grown-ups

Sunny Star Adventure is a free 3D game for children that runs in a web browser.

What children do in the game

  • Explore cheerful worlds, collect stars and coins, dress up their character and look after a pet.
  • Drive, sail, fly a helicopter and do simple jobs (delivering pizza and letters, putting out pretend fires, picking carrots) to earn in-game coins.
  • Learn a short Bible memory verse (King James Version) before each new level opens.
  • There is no fighting or scary content. Falling or bumping into a slime just sends the character back to a checkpoint.

Safety by design

  • No accounts, no passwords, no email. Nothing to sign up for.
  • No real names. Each player gets a made-up name such as "Bouncy Panda".
  • No chat. In online play, children can only send six picture stickers (👋 ⭐ 👉 🆘 🎉 ❤️). There is no typing to other players.
  • Private rooms. Online play only works with a room code that a child shares with friends they already know.
  • Coins can't be bought. There are no in-app purchases. Coins are only earned by playing.

Advertising

The game may show a short ad at natural breaks: between levels, after a "try again", or when going back to the title screen. It never shows one while your child is playing, and there are no "watch an ad for a reward" buttons. Ads are requested as child-directed and non-personalized, so they are not based on your child's interests or browsing.
